## New-entry decision record

For each newly retrieved work, record why it was pursued, whether retrieval and
direct inspection succeeded, what it changed, what it failed to resolve, and
why the next source follows. Dead links, incomplete scans, and negative archive
responses remain catalog entries rather than disappearing from the history.
