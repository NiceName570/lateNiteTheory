## Working rules

- Primary evidence, later testimony, assumptions, and computed results remain
  labeled separately.
- Every simulation has an immutable input, hash, attempt journal, raw output,
  parsed output, and a bounded statement of what it establishes.
- Change one declared variable at a time unless a run is explicitly a combined
  confirmation after single-axis tests.
- Failed attempts remain in the record.
- No old file is moved or renamed until a path-and-hash map exists.
- Electrical behavior does not establish therapeutic efficacy.
