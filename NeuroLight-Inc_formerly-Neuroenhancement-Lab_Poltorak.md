# NeuroLight, Inc. (formerly Neuroenhancement Lab)

- Address: Montebello Park, 75 Montebello Road, Suite 104, Suffern, NY 10901
- Founded 2017; rebranded NeuroLight Oct 2022 (patent name change recorded July 23, 2024)
- Founder/President: Alexander Poltorak (sole inventor on its patents)
- Reported funding: $26M
- NSF SBIR Phase I (2022): $255,851 - Neuromodulation Device to Promote Healthy Sleeping Cycle
- States it develops technology to transplant brain states between people using light and sound; first target insomnia
